Our Story

From a dream to one of Lahore's most celebrated dining destinations.

A Legacy of Passion

Velvet & Gold was born in 2018 from a simple yet powerful vision: to create a space where exceptional food, warm hospitality, and timeless elegance converge into an unforgettable experience.

What began as a modest 12-table bistro in the heart of Gulberg has grown into one of Lahore's most sought-after fine dining establishments — without ever losing the intimacy and soul that defined our earliest days.

"We don't just serve meals. We craft memories that linger long after the last bite."

Every element of Velvet & Gold — from the hand-selected marble floors to the custom-blended spice mixes in our kitchen — reflects a commitment to excellence that borders on obsession. Our team of 28 culinary and service professionals shares a singular mission: to make every guest feel like the most important person in the room.

Meet Our Culinary Team

Chef Ahmed Raza

Executive Chef

With 18 years of experience across Michelin-starred kitchens in London and Dubai, Chef Ahmed brings a rare blend of classical French technique and deep respect for Pakistani flavors.

Chef Sara Malik

Pastry Chef

Trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris, Sara transforms desserts into edible art. Her chocolate work has been featured in international culinary magazines.

Chef Omar Farooq

Sous Chef

A rising star in Pakistan's culinary scene, Omar leads our grill station with precision and creativity, specializing in wood-fired meats and seafood.
